# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Architects, senior engineers, maintainers and coding agents inspecting a
local Architecture World. Their primary job is to understand the current
architecture, its evidence, uncertainty and governance state before taking a
traceable action.

## Product Purpose

ArchSkillKit turns architecture knowledge into an inspectable, evidence-bound
local product surface. The Control Plane is an Architecture Explorer,
Governance Console and Viewer Hub; it is not an IDE or a proprietary diagram
editor. Success means a user can understand what is known, why it is known,
what remains uncertain and which safe action to take next.

## Brand Personality

Sober, precise and evidence-first. The interface should feel like a reliable
engineering instrument: calm under investigation, explicit about uncertainty
and never decorative at the expense of provenance.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards that turn every state into a colourful metric card.
- Architecture views that hide unknowns or provenance behind secondary pages.
- Omnipresent chat interfaces that replace contextual investigation actions.
- A graph-editor experience that suggests diagram edits directly mutate the
  Architecture World.

## Design Principles

1. Put evidence, confidence and origin beside the architecture fact they
   justify.
2. Make unknowns and low coverage visible; false confidence is a product
   failure.
3. Use progressive disclosure: orient a human first, then reveal raw graph,
   run and provenance detail on demand.
4. Preserve local trust boundaries: inspect by default; mutations must be
   explicit, traceable and opt-in.
5. Prefer a small number of precise, contextual actions over a general chat
   surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The Control Plane targets WCAG 2.2 AA. All core workflows must be keyboard
operable, state and provenance must not rely on colour alone, and motion must
respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
